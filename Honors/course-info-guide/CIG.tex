\section*{Course Description}
\vspace{-1em}

Physics is the study of how the universe works on the most fundamental level.  In this 
class, you will use your powers of observation and logical as well as mathematical 
reasoning to describe, explain, and predict the behavior of objects.

\vspace{-1em}

\paragraph{Course Goals} 
As a result of taking Honors Physics I, you will:

\vspace{-0.5em}


\begin{enumerate}
	\item Know and understand the process that is used to make the scientific discoveries that 
				are reported in the media or in the workplace
	\item Understand the scientific principles that underlie motion, forces, waves, and electricity.
	\item Apply your mathematical skills to solving real-world problems
	\item Develop problem solving and scientific literacy skills
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Methods of Evaluation}
\vspace{-1em}


Your pre-exam grade will be based on the methods of 
evaluation shown below. The weighting of each of these 
categories is subject to change. Your overall class 
grade will consist of the pre-exam grade (85\%) and the
final exam grade (15\%).

\begin{enumerate}
	\item	Homework (30\%)
	\begin{itemize}
		\item 
			Homework plays an important role in helping you understand the material.  Therefore, it is important that it gets done in a timely manner! 
		\item 
			There will be multiple HW quizzes each unit based upon the problems you were to complete on the syllabus. They are also open-note!  Therefore, it is to your advantage to bring to class your homework!
		\item 
			Homework is accepted for full credit until Test Day.  There will be a 50\% penalty for homework turned in after test day.  Homework will not be accepted any later than the Test for the following unit.
	\end{itemize}
	\item	Labs/Projects (30\%)
	\begin{itemize}
		\item 
			Many labs will have an associated worksheet to turn in or a short summary paragraph to complete via Schoology.  
		\item 
			A few labs will require a fully typed lab write-up to be uploaded to Schoology.
		\item 
			Late labs will be assessed a penalty of 10\% per school day (whether or not class meets) up to 50\%.  Labs that are more than a month late will not be accepted.
		\item 
			There will be one research project in the Spring Semester.  If you complete your research project late, it is immediately assessed a 50\% penalty.
	\end{itemize}
	\item	Tests (40\%)
	\begin{itemize}
		\item	
			Each chapter will end with a quiz or a test.  Equations for that unit will be provided.
		\item 
			Notes or other aids will not be accessible during the test.
	\end{itemize}
	
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Artificial Intelligence}
	You are being evaluated on how well \emph{you} can meet the course objectives, not on how well AI can do so. Here are some guidelines:

	\begin{enumerate}
		\item AI use is \textbf{entirely forbidden} on tests, quizzes and lab reports, because these assignments are designed to showcase your thinking and not a computer's thinking.  AI use in these situations will be considered Academic Dishonesty and will result in a zero and a detention.
		\item Using AI to help you prepare for a test by giving practice questions or explaining topics is permitted.
		\item Using AI for hints on particularly difficult HW problems can be okay as long as you are using it \emph{sparingly} to \emph{aid in your own understanding}. If you use AI for help on your homework, \textbf{\emph{you are required to indicate that you have done so}}. For example, you can write, ``ChatGPT helped me set up this problem,'' next to your work.  Remember, you will need to be able to do problems on your own by the time you get to the test, so don't let yourself rely too heavily on AI.
		\item Mr. Rohrbach is a better teacher than AI.  If you find yourself relying on AI during homework, he would be happy to help you out during SRT, in class, or after school.  
	\end{enumerate}
