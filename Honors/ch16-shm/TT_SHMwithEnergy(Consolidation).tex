\question 
  A mass on a spring has a \emph{period} of 0.7~seconds and an amplitude of 1.2~meters.

  \begin{parts}
    \part Write down its equation of motion $x(t)$.
    \part Calculate the total energy of this system.
    \part Calculate the maximum speed of the mass.
  \end{parts}

  \vs

\question 
  An object with mass 1.8 kg is attached to a spring with spring constant $k=250$~N/m and executes simple harmonic motion. At the moment when the object is 0.030~m from its equilibrium position, its speed is 0.40~m/s.  Write the equation for motion $x(t)$.
