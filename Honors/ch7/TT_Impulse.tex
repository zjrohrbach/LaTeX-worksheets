\question 
    A baseball is thrown by a pitcher with a velocity of 43 m/s. The batter hits {\bf straight back} at the pitcher with a velocity of 56 m/s. If the ball was in contact with the bat for 0.0057~seconds and has a mass of 0.145~kg, what is the force on the baseball?

\question
  While washing your car, water comes out of the hose at a rate of 1.5 kg/s and a speed of 20 m/s.  How much force does the water impart to your car?


\vs \hrule \vs  

\question
  Rain is falling at a rate of of 2.5~cm/hr and accumulates in a pan.  If the raindrops hit at 8.0~m/s, estimate the force on the bottom of the 1.0-square-meter pan due to the impacting rain which we assume does not rebound.  The density of water is \SI{1000}{kg/m^3}.
