\newcommand{\prob}{

  \noindent
  Sara, an amazingly well-rounded athlete, participates in a grueling race.

 \begin{questions}
 
  \question 
      Sara begins the race by running 5~km from the starting line to checkpoint~A in 20 minutues and 40 seconds.  What is her average velocity (in m/s)?
    \question
      Next, Sara swims \SI{500}{\meter} from checkpoint~A to checkpoint~B with a velocity of \SI{1.1}{\meter\per\second}.  How much time does it take her?
    \question
      From checkpoint~B she rides a bike with a velocity of 4.9 m/s for 1~hour.  What is the distance between checkpoint~B to checkpoint~C?
    \question
      Sara must now get all the way back to the starting line to finish the race.  Completely exhausted, Sara climbs into her Jeep and drives back to the starting line.  What is her velocity to go from checkpoint~C to the starting line if the Jeep ride is 19 minutes and 17 seconds?
    \question
      Calculate Sara's {\bf average speed} and {\bf average velocity} for the entire race.
 \end{questions}
}
