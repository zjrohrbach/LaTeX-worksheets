{\LARGE A 6-kg pool ball moving due east at 3~m/s collides with a second 4-kg ball initially at rest.  After the collision, the first ball moves 2.3 m/s at a direction 40$^\circ$ north of east.  Find the magnitude and direction of the velocity of the second ball.}
