\section*{Practice}

\begin{questions}
  \question 
    A cable lifts a 2600-kg elevator a distance of 30~m in a time of 8 seconds.  
    
    \begin{parts}
      \part What power does the motor output in accomplishing this task?  Express in horsepower. \vs

      \part If the time to lift the elevator doubled to 16~seconds, what would the power output be? \vs

      \part In which case was more work done: question (a) or question (b)? Explain. \vs
      
    \end{parts}
  \pagebreak 
  \question
    Two cars travel 12 km.  They travel against identical friction and air drag forces totalling 2500~N.  Car~A travels at a constant speed of 30~m/s; Car~B travels at a constant speed of 45~m/s.

    \begin{parts}
      \part
        Which car's engine exerted more work?
        \vs
      \part
        Which car's engine had a greater output of power?
        \vs
      \part
        Calculate each car's power output in horsepower.
        \vs[2]
    \end{parts}

      
    \question
      A compact fluorescent 60-watt replacement bulb only requires~13 W of power. Over the course of an hour, how many Joules of energy are saved?
      \vs[3]


  \question
    You get an electric bill for 50 kWh (killowatt-hours). How much electrical work was done by your house in Joules.
    \vs[3]
  
\end{questions}
