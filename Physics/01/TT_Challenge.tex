\question 
You leave from home on a road trip at \textbf{noon}.  You are driving steadily at 25~m/s for \textbf{180~km}.  After that distance, it begins to rain, so you slow down to 18~m/s for the rest of the trip.  At \textbf{4:30pm}, you arrive at your destination.  How far is your destination from home?

{\small Adapted from problem 2-7 in Giancolli \emph{Physics: Principles and Applications}, 7th edition.}

\begin{solution}
  7200 seconds at 25 m/s; 9000 seconds at 18 m/s; 162 km a 18 m/s; \textbf{342 km total}
\end{solution}

\question Two bicycles start moving, but one has a starting position that is ahead of the other. The front bicycle travels at a constant speed of 12 m/s, while the back bicycle travels at a constant speed of 18 m/s. The initial distance between the two bicycles is 150 meters.  Calculate where the back bicycle overtakes the front bicycle.

\begin{solution}
  300 m from front bicycle or 450 m from back bicycle.
\end{solution}

\question
Two cars start a distance of 100~meters apart, facing each other.  Car A travels east at 10 m/s; Car B travels west at 15 m/s.

  \vspace{1em}

  \noindent
  Superfly is sitting on the front bumper of Car A.  He has super powers that allow him to fly at a constant speed of 45~m/s and instantaneously change direction.  Superfly flies toward the bumper of Car B and then instantaneously turns around and flies back toward Car A.  He continues to go back and forth between the two cars until they collide.  (Superfly is also invincible.)

  \vspace{1em}

  \noindent
  What distance does Superfly travel before the cars collide?

  \begin{solution}
    $t=d/(v_1+v_2) = 4$ sec;  $v_3 t = v_3 d/(v_1+v_2) = 180$ m;

    displacement $v_1 d/(v_1+v_2) = 28.6$ m
  \end{solution}
