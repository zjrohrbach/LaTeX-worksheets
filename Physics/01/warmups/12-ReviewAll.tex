\def\content{

Let's do a problem that puts together almost everything you've learned in this unit:

\vspace{1em}

\noindent
Consider a bicycle traveling at an initial velocity of \SI{-2.3}{m/s}.  It accelerates constantly at a rate of \SI{-0.4}{m/s^2} for \SI{5.1}{s}.

\vspace{1em}

\begin{parts}

  \part Draw the velocity and acceleration vectors.

  \begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.5]
    \draw pic[scale=0.5,x=-1cm]{bike};
    \draw (-10,-1) rectangle ++(20,-0.2);
  \end{tikzpicture}

  \part Is the bicycle traveling to the right or to the left? How do you know? \vspace{2em}

  \part Is it speeding up or slowing down?  How do you know? \vspace{2em}

  \part Sketch the displacement and velocity graphs for the bike's motion.
  %
  \blankgraphs

  \part Set up your knowns and unknowns and then solve for the final velocity.
  
\end{parts}



}
