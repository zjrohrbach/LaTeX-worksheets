\begin{questions}
  
    \question
      You and a friend are able to use a rope to pull a giant cement statue across the ground with an acceleration of 0.07 m/s$^2$.  If you apply a force of 500 N, your friend applies a force of 480 N, and the force of friction on the block is 300 N, what is the mass of the statue?
      \vs

    \question
      Neal is stealing a refrigerator (173 kg) from the bank.  If the force of friction on the refrigerator is 254~N, what force must he apply to have an acceleration of 1.27 m/s$^2$?
      \vs

    \question
      A loaded semi truck has a mass of \SI{36000}{kg}.  Its engine applies a forward force of \SI{20820}{N}, causing it to accelerate at \SI{0.12}{m/s^2}.  Calculate the force of friction on the truck.
      \vs

  

\end{questions}
