\def\content{
The purpose of this warmup is to make sure that we are confident calculating period and frequency.  Make sure to include units!

\begin{questions}
  
  \question
  In order to capture video, a video camera takes 32 pictures per second.  

  \begin{parts}
    \part 
      What is the period?
      \vs
    \part 
      What is the frequency with which the camera takes pictures?
      \vs

  \end{parts}

  \question
  A flautist in the marching band makes 120 steps in one minute (60 seconds). 
  \begin{parts}
    \part 
      What is the period of the flautist's steps?
      \vs
    \part 
      What is the frequency?
      \vs
  \end{parts}

\end{questions}
}
