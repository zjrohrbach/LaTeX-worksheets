\question
  A marble starts at rest at the top of a frictionless ramp with 80~J of potential energy.  At the bottom of the ramp, the ball is going 12~m/s.  Use proportional reasoning to find the velocity of the ball when it is only 1/4 of the way down the ramp.  Use the image below to help you
